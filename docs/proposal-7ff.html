The problem worth solving

You have 10,000+ affiliates and a Skool community. In most affiliate programs a small group sends the deals and the rest sign up, send one or two, then go quiet. Nobody is doing anything wrong. There just aren't enough people to check in with thousands of partners every two weeks, remember who said "check back after tax season," and bring back the ones who drifted away.

Each affiliate who comes back sends more deals. The job is keeping every one of those relationships warm, at 10,000-person scale, without spamming anyone and without losing the personal touch.

What we run for you

This is the full operating system, built inside your GoHighLevel (the workflow map covers every piece):

Every affiliate, every two weeks

An SMS or email touch every two weeks, an offer once a month, and a newsletter on the 1st and 15th. Everyone is tagged by where they came from and where they are in the lifecycle.

Real conversations, followed up on the day

"How's it going? What can I help with? Seen the new programs?" When someone says "check back with me," it becomes a note and a task in GoHighLevel, and it shows up on that morning's list.

Quiet and dead affiliates brought back

A daily look at everyone silent for 30, 60, and 90+ days. Personal check-ins for past producers, and reactivation campaigns for the rest.

Events that feed the program

Regular partner trainings on how to use the program and get more leads: invites, reminders, replays, and follow-up with everyone who showed interest.

New offers, launched properly

New programs and products go to the segments they fit, with the copy approved by your team first.

The truth, weekly

A scorecard from the CRM: who's producing, who replied, who's slipping, what went out, and what came back.

Guardrails

  • A human approves every message and every campaign before it goes out. The agent cannot approve its own sends.
  • Your partner lead signs off on offer copy and anything about program terms. Commissions and payouts are never changed by us or the agent.
  • No approval, rate, or income promises. "Guaranteed" and "no credit check" are blocked in the send path.
  • SMS only to opted-in contacts on registered numbers, with opt-out language and quiet hours. STOP means never contacted again.
  • One command freezes all outbound. Every action is audited.
