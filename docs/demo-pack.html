      Rules the code enforces
      
              
        
            • A person reads and approves every message. Each batch has a review file listing every text in full, and any of them can be held back.
              • 
        
            • Texts go out in the partner manager's name and voice. They never mention AI, and "is this a bot?" is never answered by the agent: the manager gets a task and replies personally.
              • 
        
            • Kickoffs stay within two SMS segments, ask exactly one question, and never guilt anyone for being quiet. STOP in any form is final.
              • 
        
            • Calls come in from any recorder: GoHighLevel phone calls by transcript, video calls by the recorder's export. Every recorded call opens with a recording notice.
              • 
        
            • No health details, religion, politics, account or ID numbers, or passwords ever enter a profile.

            The problem worth solving
            
  
            7FF has more than 13,000 partners, most of them in Zoho. About 50 are active and sending deals. That leaves roughly 12,950 people who signed up, maybe sent a deal or two, and went quiet. Nobody did anything wrong. There just aren't enough people to check in with thousands of partners every two weeks, remember who said "check back after tax season," and bring back the ones who drifted.
            
  
            Every partner who comes back sends deals that would never have arrived, and some will want the Funding Machine for themselves. The job is warming those relationships back up at 13,000-person scale, without spamming anyone and without losing the personal touch.
            

            


            
  
            The goal: a relationship with every partner
            
  
            The reactivation text is only the start. The goal is a lasting relationship with each partner:
            
  
              
    
            1. Open the door to a real relationship with each partner.
              2. 
    
            2. Build a partner profile for each one: tier, history, niche, goals, and every conversation.
              3. 
    
            3. Know each partner. The agent learns each partner's specifics from their profile and every conversation, so every message fits that person.
              4. 
    
            4. Plan a revenue channel. For every partner who engages, a plan for turning them into a steady source of deals.
              5. 
    
            5. Coach the community. In time, offer affiliate coaching in the group, helping members grow their own affiliate and referral programs.
              6. 
  
            
  
            
    
            Phase 1 · starting this month
            Reactivation kickoff
            One personal text to each quiet partner, then a real back-and-forth with everyone who answers. A pilot of 250 first, then 2,500, then the remaining 10,200, each wave opening only when the one before it has worked.
            
    
            Phase 2 · the first 90 days
            The kickstart
            Relationships take time. We build every partner's profile, learn each partner from every conversation, and put a revenue plan in place for everyone who engages. At day 90 we review the results together, with real data.
            
    
            Phase 3 · 6 to 12 months
            Ongoing affiliate management
            Every partner on a steady cadence, with revenue compounding as more partners come back. We refine the program together, then release it to the whole community in a controlled way.
            
  
            

            


            
  
            What we run for you
            
  
            The full operating system, built in GoHighLevel (the workflow map covers every piece):
            
  
            
    
            One personal text to every quiet partner
            A kickoff written for each partner from their own record (name, tier, last deal, niche), then a real back-and-forth with everyone who answers.
            
    
            Every partner, every two weeks
            A text or email touch every two weeks, an offer once a month, and a newsletter on the 1st and 15th. Everyone is tagged by tier, source and where they are in the lifecycle.
            
    
            Follow-ups on the day promised
            When someone says "check back with me," it becomes a note and a task in GoHighLevel and shows up on that morning's list. A partner with a deal gets a person on the phone the same day.
            
    
            Events that feed the program
            Regular partner trainings on using the program and getting more leads: invites, reminders, replays, and follow-up with everyone who showed interest.
            
    
            New offers, launched properly
            New programs and products, the Funding Machine included, go to the partners they fit, with copy approved by your team first.
            
    
            The truth, weekly
            A scorecard from the CRM: who replied, who opted out, deals by partner tier, and AI spend against the budget.

            What runs it
            
  
            A Hermes agent connected to GoHighLevel. Texts and replies are written by GPT-6 Luna Pro through OpenRouter, with GLM 5.3 Flash (open weights) as the backup. Jev reads every partner record, checks every draft, and classifies every reply. Luna Pro and GLM 5.3 Flash cost $0.50 per million output tokens, and Jev charges only for what it reads, which is why the AI line stays in the tens of dollars.
            
  
            Partner records are read from Zoho, never written back. Partners with payroll integrations stay in Zoho untouched.

            How it's built
            
  
            One agent with its own server, its own computer, and a memory you can open, working inside your GoHighLevel under a human's approval.
            
  
            
    
            
      
            Brain
            Hermes agent on its own server. For 7FF: GPT-6 Luna Pro through OpenRouter, GLM 5.3 Flash (open weights) as the fallback, and Jev to read, check and classify
            
      
            Hands
            Its own Orgo cloud desktop, with a browser for the GoHighLevel screens, LinkedIn and Skool
            
      
            CRM
            GoHighLevel through the API: contacts, tags, fields, notes, tasks, conversations, workflows, pipelines
            
      
            Research
            Super Browser and Browser Box. The agent researches affiliates itself; no paid data vendors
            
      
            Memory
            An Obsidian vault: a profile note for every partner, linked to their tier, line of work, manager and what their clients need, plus a note for every call, every day and every campaign
            
      
            Channel
            A Slack app where the manager talks to it and approves each morning's card
            
    
            
    
            Always a human's call
              
      
            • Every message and every campaign enrollment is frozen, shown on an approval card, and sent only after a person approves it. The agent cannot approve its own sends.
              • 
      
            • Recurring cadence can run under a standing approval that has a daily cap and an expiry, and can be revoked at any time.
              • 
      
            • Commissions, payouts and program terms are never changed by the agent.
              • 
      
            • No approval, rate or income promises. "Guaranteed" and "no credit check" are blocked before anything sends.
              • 
      
            • SMS carries opt-out language and only goes out during daytime hours. STOP means never contacted again.
              • 
      
            • One command freezes all outbound. Every action is logged.

            Source and reruns
            
  
            Every number in this pack reruns from source: the costs from a cost model built on published prices, and the simulation from a fixed seed, so the same inputs always give the same numbers.
            
  
            python3 scripts/cost_model.py
fam sim seed --count 250 --start 2026-09-01 --out sim-output
fam sim run  --days 30  --start 2026-09-01 --out sim-output
python3 scripts/build_demo_pack.py --sim sim-output
