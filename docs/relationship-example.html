Where it lives

Three layers, each with one job. 7FF's partner data stays in 7FF's vault and CRM: no graph database and no outside service holds it.

Memory you can open

Obsidian vault

One note per partner, linked to their tier, what they do, their manager, where they came from, and the products their clients need. Obsidian's graph view is the relationship graph. Calls, review files and a daily log sit alongside.

Answers questions

The index

Every fact with its source and date, every interaction, every hub link. It orders the kickoff waves and answers questions like "Executive partners whose clients need equipment financing." It can be rebuilt from the notes at any time.

What the team sees

GoHighLevel fields

Tier, a short profile summary, how complete the profile is, the next step, and a link to the note, on every contact, so the team works from the same picture.

Rules the code enforces
  • A person reads and approves every message. Each batch has a review file listing every text in full, and any of them can be held back.
  • Texts go out in the partner manager's name and voice. They never mention AI, and "is this a bot?" is never answered by the agent: the manager gets a task and replies personally.
  • Kickoffs stay within two SMS segments, ask exactly one question, and never guilt anyone for being quiet. STOP in any form is final.
  • Calls come in from any recorder: GoHighLevel phone calls by transcript, video calls by the recorder's export. Every recorded call opens with a recording notice.
  • No health details, religion, politics, account or ID numbers, or passwords ever enter a profile.
