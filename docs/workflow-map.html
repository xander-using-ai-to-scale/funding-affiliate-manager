The daily loop: a short list and ready drafts by 8am

This runs every weekday on a schedule. The manager's job shrinks to reading one card, editing if needed, and approving.

  1. Health check

    GoHighLevel connected, outbound not frozen, today's send counts.agent · automatic

  2. Sweep and retag

    Every affiliate is scored for silence. Lifecycle tags and pipeline stages are updated. Silent 30+ and 90+ are counted.agent · automatic, audited

  3. Build today's queue

    "Check back with me" follow-ups first, then 60+ day check-ins, with past producers ranked highest. Capped at 40 per day; the rest roll over.agent · automatic

  4. Draft personally

    One message per person, from their profile, niche, source, last deal, and their own last words to us.agent

  5. One approval card

    The whole batch is frozen with a digest and posted to Slack. Nothing leaves until a human approves it.manager · one click

  6. Send and record

    Messages go out, last-touch dates update, follow-up tasks close, and the day is written to the Obsidian vault.agent

  7. Bulk cadence

    Bi-weekly touches, monthly offers, and reactivation go by workflow enrollment, under a standing approval with a daily cap that expires.agent · within manager's limits

What gets built in GoHighLevel

The whole system is defined as data in the repo, so a new client gets the same build from one snapshot and one installer.

Affiliate ID, join date, source, niche, audience size, deals submitted, last deal, funded volume, last touch (and channel), last offer, last reply, next touch, cadence note, preferred channel, assigned manager, products of interest, growth services, vault note.

Affiliate Lifecycle: Applied → Onboarded → Active → Producing → Quiet → Dormant → Reactivated.
Affiliate Growth Services: Interested → Scoped → In Progress → Delivered.

Guardrails, built in

AUTONOMOUS
Reading, sweeping, lifecycle tags, notes, follow-up tasks. Every change is audited.
HUMAN APPROVES
Every message and every workflow enrollment: frozen, digested, approved once, then executed. The agent cannot approve its own sends.
BOUNDED
Recurring cadence runs under a standing approval with a daily cap and an expiry, revocable instantly. One command freezes all outbound.
NEVER
Guaranteed approvals, rates, or income claims; changing commissions or payouts; messaging opted-out contacts; paid data-enrichment vendors.

Compliance built into the send path: SMS carries opt-out language and only goes out 11:00–20:00 ET, STOP means do-not-contact forever, and a lint blocks "guaranteed", "no credit check", and stated rates. The demo runs in demo mode, where only designated test contacts receive real messages.
