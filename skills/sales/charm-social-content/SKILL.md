# Charm Offensive — Social Content

Write organic social posts that build influence the Charm way: win attention with a pattern interrupt,
be human and honest, give value ("ideas you're free to steal"), and let personality do the selling.

## Step 0 — Load voice, story, and profile (always)

1. Load `skill_view(name='charm-offensive-foundation', file_path='references/voice-and-humour.md')` — the
   ten humour formulas and voice rules are the toolkit for hooks and lines.
2. Load `skill_view(name='charm-offensive-foundation', file_path='references/story-formula.md')` — the
   Identity→Struggle→Discovery→Surprise arc turns the user's experiences into scroll-stopping posts.
3. Get the user's `charm-profile.md` (story, what they stand for/against, offer, voice assets). Their
   "what you stand for / against" and their obstacles are rich post fuel. Build one via
   `charm-offensive-foundation` if missing.

## Step 1 — Clarify the brief

Confirm in one short batch: platform (LinkedIn / X / Skool / etc.), goal (grow audience, warm prospects,
promote something, share a story), topic or raw material (a lesson, a win, a hot take, a story), how
many posts, and any hard no-go topics.

## Step 2 — Choose the shape

Pick the format that fits the goal:

- **Story post** — run the Identity→Struggle→Discovery→Surprise arc. Great for lessons and origin
  stories. Open with a curiosity-gap hook (first line must earn the "…see more" click).
- **Value / "steal this" post** — a genuinely useful idea or mini-framework, given freely. Frame as
  a gift, not a teaser for a pitch.
- **Honest hot take** — lean on what the user "stands for / against" in their sector. Disarming
  honesty + a clear point of view.
- **Humour / pattern-interrupt post** — use a humour formula (the Reverse, an absurd list with one
  ridiculous item, a parody, a meme concept). Memes pack a whole premise into an image so the caption
  lands the punch.

## Step 3 — Write

- **First line is everything** — it's the hook; make it a genuine pattern interrupt, not "I'm excited
  to share…". Use a humour formula, a bold honest admission, or a curiosity gap.
- Short lines, white space, scannable. No jargon, no corporate throat-clearing.
- One idea per post. Give value or tell a real story; let the personality sell.
- Soft or no CTA (a question to spark comments, or "steal this"). Avoid hard-sell CTAs.
- Everything true — real stories, real opinions. Never fabricate.

## Step 4 — Self-check and deliver

Run the voice self-check. Then: does the first line stop the scroll? One clear idea? Human and funny
without trying too hard? Value given freely? True? Deliver the post(s), each with 2–3 alternate hooks
so the user can pick, plus any image/meme concept described. Offer to draft a batch or a week's
calendar. Never auto-post without approval.
