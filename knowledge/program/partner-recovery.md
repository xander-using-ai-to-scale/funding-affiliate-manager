# Runbook: recovering an unhappy or stuck affiliate

1. Stop automation for them. Do not enroll them in cadence workflows until this is resolved (note it on the contact).
2. Read the full history (`fam_affiliate_brief` + their vault note) and find the real blocker.
3. Classify it: access | assets | timing | offer confusion | deal outcome | commission/payout | system failure | no response | dissatisfaction.
4. Recommend one human recovery action to the manager, with a pre-call brief.
5. Record every attempt and the affiliate's preferred next step (`fam_note_add`, `fam_followup_schedule`).
6. Escalate immediately, with no further outbound, for anything legal, reputational, hostile, or consent-related.

Commission or payout questions: acknowledge only. Never calculate or promise a payment. The human owner answers from the approved source.
