# Operating cadence — funding affiliate program

Adapted from our prior affiliate-program deployments (affiliate-program-launch playbook and the My Connected Med affiliate manager).

## Daily (agent + Funding Relationship Manager)
- 08:00 ET: sweep + lifecycle retag, then the morning task queue ("check back" follow-ups first, then 60-day check-ins). The manager approves one batch card.
- Hourly 09:15–18:15 ET: reply triage. 🔥 `has_deal` gets a same-day call. Commission questions go to the human.
- End of day: summary in #funding-affiliates and the vault daily note.

## Weekly
- Mon: pipeline review. Who's producing, who went quiet, and new applicants still in onboarding.
- Tue: growth-services queue (funnels, reactivation, email, social, CRM help). Owners and next steps.
- Wed: offers and content. Next monthly offer, newsletter draft (on newsletter weeks), social kit.
- Thu: partner training / Skool coaching call. Invites, reminders, follow-ups.
- Fri 16:00: scorecard (routines/weekly-scorecard.prompt), decisions needed, and next week's commitments.

## Every two weeks
- Newsletter (1st and 15th). Every affiliate also gets a bi-weekly touch, alternating SMS and email.

## Monthly
- Monthly offer (3rd). Cohort review: quiet → dormant → dead movement and reactivation rate.
- Source review: which sources (Skool, LinkedIn, webinars, referrals) produce active affiliates.

## Quarterly
- Dead-lead reactivation campaign. Program review with the client: activity, deals, services delivered.

## Escalation clocks
- **Immediate** (pause automation for that affiliate, alert a human): legal threats, public complaints, consent/STOP violations, anything involving an applicant's sensitive documents.
- **Same business day**: an affiliate with a deal, a commission or payout dispute, a high-value affiliate going quiet.
- **Standard (48h)**: routine questions, service requests, content asks.
