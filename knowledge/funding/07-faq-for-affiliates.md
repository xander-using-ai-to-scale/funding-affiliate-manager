# 07 — FAQ for Affiliates

20 likely questions with answer frameworks. Where 7FF-specific facts are
unknown, the framework uses a placeholder — fill it from a verified source
before repeating it, never guess.

**1. "What's my commission?"**
Framework: "I don't want to guess and get you the wrong number — let me
confirm the current rate (`{{COMMISSION_RATE}}`) and get back to you." Never
state a percentage from memory (`03-affiliate-economics.md`).

**2. "When do I get paid?"**
Framework: "Commissions are paid on Fridays for everything earned through
the preceding Tuesday at 11:59 PM MST. On your first commission you'll get
an invite to our payroll system, Everee — once you set that up, payment
lands within 7 days." (Verified — see `03-affiliate-economics.md`.)

**3. "What happens if I don't set up Everee?"**
Framework: "If Everee isn't set up within 6 months of the invite, unpaid
commissions are forfeited — so it's worth doing as soon as you get that
invite."

**4. "How do I submit a deal / refer a client?"**
Framework: "Send them through your link (`{{AFFILIATE_LINK}}`) — they fill
out a short application (soft credit check only), then a Funding Advisor
calls to walk through options."

**5. "Will this hurt my client's credit?"**
Framework: "Pre-qualifying is a soft pull, so no. If they move forward with
a specific offer, that lender may do a hard pull at that stage — and if
they're doing something like stacking multiple cards, each issuer
application is its own hard inquiry."

**6. "What products do you offer?"**
Framework: point them to `02-funding-products.md`'s [7FF-LISTED] items
(0% intro business credit cards, business line of credit, business-purpose
personal term loan, business term loan, SBA, business-use HELOC) or the live
marketplace page — don't recite numbers from memory since the two public
pages disagree on at least one rate range.

**7. "How fast can my client get funded?"**
Framework: "Depends on the product — some can move in days, and 7FF has
said some options can fund in as little as 48 hours. That's not a promise
for any specific client though; it depends on their profile and the
product." Never repeat "48 hours" as a guarantee.

**8. "Does my client need good credit?"**
Framework: "It varies by product — some (like the 0% card strategy) lean
more on personal credit, others weigh business revenue more. I can't
qualify them myself, but there's no harm in having them go through the
soft-pull application to see what comes back."

**9. "Do I need an LLC or business entity to be a partner?"**
`{{PARTNER_ENTITY_REQUIREMENT}}` — **UNVERIFIED.** This exact question
appears as an FAQ header on 7FF's homepage, but the answer content wasn't
retrievable from the public page. Confirm with 7FF before answering.

**10. "Do you offer personal funding, not just business?"**
`{{PERSONAL_FUNDING_ANSWER}}` — **UNVERIFIED** for the same reason as #9;
also note that 7FF's marketplace does list some products (0% cards, personal
term loan) that are technically consumer-credit products used for a
business purpose — see `02-funding-products.md` — which may be the actual
answer, but confirm before stating it as such.

**11. "Is there a cost for me to join or use the partner program?"**
Framework: "No — joining and using the partner program is free. 7FF's own
language is that they'd never charge you for sending them leads."
(Verified.)

**12. "Is this a business opportunity / will I make money just for signing
people up under me?"**
Framework: "No — this is a straightforward affiliate/referral program, not
a business opportunity or multi-level structure. You earn on deals you
personally refer that get funded, that's it." (Keep this framing intact per
`06-compliance-guardrails.md`.)

**13. "Can I get my client a discount?"**
Framework: "You can request one — it comes out of your own commission on
that deal, up to your commission percentage, and 7FF has to approve it.
Requests go to info@7figures.com." (Verified mechanic; never state the
percentage from memory.)

**14. "I haven't sent a deal in a while — is my account still active?"**
Framework: acknowledge, don't assume a reason, offer help — see the T4/T5
reactivation frameworks in `05-conversation-playbook.md`. Do not state a
specific "still active" status unless confirmed in a system of record.

**15. "Can you help me build a funnel page / work my old leads / run email
or social / get a CRM going?"**
Framework: point to the growth-service offer (T8 in
`05-conversation-playbook.md`) and get specifics on which one they want,
then route to a human to scope it.

**16. "How do I track my referrals / see deal status?"**
Framework: "You've got the Partner CRM for tracking submissions, plus a
Slack integration for real-time decision updates." **UNVERIFIED — confirm
with 7FF** exactly what this agent itself can see/pull from those systems
versus what only the affiliate can see directly.

**17. "Can I promote 7FF on social media / my website?"**
Framework: "Yes, and just make sure you disclose the affiliate relationship
clearly when you do (the FTC expects that) — happy to point you to the
current approved claims/disclosure language once we have it finalized."
(See `06-compliance-guardrails.md` — don't draft exact disclosure wording
without sign-off.)

**18. "What if my client already has other funding / existing debt?"**
Framework: "Worth mentioning to the Funding Advisor upfront — especially if
they already have an active merchant cash advance or similar, since
stacking those can affect what they qualify for. I can't evaluate that
myself, but flagging it early helps."

**19. "Who do I contact if something goes wrong with a deal / I have a
complaint?"**
Framework: use the complaint response in `05-conversation-playbook.md` —
acknowledge, get detail, escalate to a human immediately, no promises about
outcome.

**20. "I want to stop getting texts/emails from you."**
Framework: honor immediately per `06-compliance-guardrails.md` (STOP / DNC
handling); single neutral acknowledgment, no argument, no "just confirming"
follow-up.
