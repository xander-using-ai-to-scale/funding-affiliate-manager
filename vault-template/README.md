# fam-vault — Funding Affiliate Manager memory

Open this folder in Obsidian. Everything the agent knows and does lands here, and the graph view is the
relationship graph: partners linked to their tier, what they do, their manager, where they came from, and
what their clients need.

- **Partners/** — one profile per partner: a short summary, what they want, how they work, open loops, their
  revenue plan, background from their record, every fact with its source and date, their calls, your
  notes, and a timeline. `## Manager notes` is yours: the agent reads it and never edits it.
- **Tiers/**, **Niches/**, **Managers/**, **Sources/**, **Products/** — hub notes the partner profiles link to.
- **Calls/** — one note per recorded call: summary, what we learned, commitments, transcript.
- **Reviews/** — every message in a batch, in full, numbered so you can approve all but a few.
- **Daily/** — one note per day: sweep results, approvals, what went out, replies, calls, deals.
- **Campaigns/**, **Newsletters/**, **Events/**, **Reports/** — drafts and final copy.
- **Affiliates/** — notes from before the relationship graph; each moves to Partners/ when it's next updated.

GoHighLevel is the system of record for contacts; this vault is the agent's memory and your audit trail.
